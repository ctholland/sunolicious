The Song

"Hard Times" is an acoustic country-soul record built on plainspoken storytelling and a chant-driven hook. The verses confront pride under pressure, while the chorus lands with a simple truth: hard times don't take sides. Stripped back and performance-forward, the song moves from tension to release, giving space for a vocal that can carry grit, restraint, and lift.

Why It Fits

The song sits naturally in the country-soul territory Teddy Swims inhabits — where raw honesty meets power. The hook is built for a voice that can deliver both edge and empathy, allowing the performance to feel lived-in rather than produced. It offers room for ownership, not imitation, and is structured to let the vocal lead the record.
