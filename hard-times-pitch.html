HARD TIMES

Original Composition Written & Produced by Christofer Holland

SRU Registered • 100% Controlled • Unreleased

The Song

"Hard Times" is an acoustic anthem about struggle, hypocrisy, and the need for compassion when the world forgets its balance. It calls out the double standards of power and pride — the ones who preach self-reliance until the storm hits their own door. Honest, raw, and unfiltered, it's a song about unity, humility, and remembering that hard times don't take sides.

Why It Fits

This song sits naturally in the emotional territory Teddy Swims commands — where grit meets tenderness. It offers room for interpretation and ownership, not imitation, and is structured to support a performance that feels personal and lived-in.
